解读力感知–免疫轴，
让胰腺癌的免疫“冷”微环境重新“热”起来 Decoding the mechano-immune tumor microenvironment to turn immune-cold pancreatic cancer hot

胰腺癌组织异常变硬。我们研究细胞力感受器 FAK 与 PYK2 如何感知这种力学变化、塑造免疫抑制微环境，并据此开发新的免疫治疗策略。 Pancreatic tumors become abnormally stiff. We study how the cellular force sensors FAK and PYK2 read this mechanical change, shape the immunosuppressive microenvironment, and how that knowledge can drive new immunotherapies.

从组织变硬，到免疫抑制From tissue stiffness to immune suppression

胰腺癌的特征是致密的纤维化间质。课题组把“力”看作影响免疫细胞命运的信号，沿这条轴线研究机制并寻找干预点。 Pancreatic cancer is defined by a dense fibrotic stroma. We treat mechanical force as a signal that shapes immune-cell fate, and follow that axis from mechanism to intervention.

姜红教授长期聚焦胰腺癌的力感受器 FAK/PYK2，先后在 Nature Medicine（2016）、Gut（2020）和 Cancer Discovery（2025）发表代表性研究，揭示力感受器如何调控胰腺癌的间质与免疫抑制微环境。 Prof. Jiang has long focused on the force sensors FAK and PYK2 in pancreatic cancer. Representative work in Nature Medicine (2016), Gut (2020) and Cancer Discovery (2025) shows how these sensors govern the stroma and the immunosuppressive microenvironment.

这些工作从“抑制 FAK 使肿瘤对免疫检查点治疗敏感”，到“FAK 抑制后的间质耗竭与耐药”，再到“PYK2 作为免疫机械力检查点调控单核细胞向巨噬细胞分化”，构成一条连贯的力感知–免疫研究主线。 The line runs from FAK inhibition sensitizing tumors to checkpoint immunotherapy, to stromal depletion and resistance after FAK inhibition, to PYK2 acting as an immunomechanical checkpoint that governs monocyte-to-macrophage differentiation.

姜红教授于 2021、2022、2023 和 2025 年入选科睿唯安“全球高被引科学家”（交叉领域）。 Prof. Jiang was named a Clarivate Highly Cited Researcher (Cross-Field) in 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2025.

学科方向涵盖肿瘤学、免疫学、研究与实验医学、胃肠病学与肝病学。姜红博士曾在四川大学、圣路易斯华盛顿大学（WUSTL）、路易斯维尔大学、南京医科大学和路易斯维尔退伍军人医疗中心学习和工作。 Subject areas: oncology, immunology, research and experimental medicine, and gastroenterology and hepatology. Dr. Hong Jiang studied and worked at Sichuan University, Washington University in St. Louis (WUSTL), the University of Louisville, Nanjing Medical University and the Louisville VA Medical Center.

PYK2 是免疫机械力检查点PYK2 is an immunomechanical checkpoint

FAK 家族成员 PYK2 作为免疫机械力检查点，感知力学信号并调控单核细胞向巨噬细胞分化，从而影响胰腺癌的免疫抑制微环境。

PYK2, a member of the FAK family, acts as an immunomechanical checkpoint. It senses mechanical cues and governs monocyte-to-macrophage differentiation, shaping the immunosuppressive microenvironment.

RIPK2 是胰腺癌的免疫治疗靶点RIPK2 is an immunotherapy target in pancreatic cancer

在小鼠胰腺癌模型中，针对激酶和膜蛋白的 CRISPR 筛选发现，肿瘤细胞内的 RIPK2 驱动免疫逃逸。阻断 RIPK2 可恢复肿瘤细胞表面的 MHC-I，改善致密的纤维化间质，并提高抗 PD-1 治疗的效果。

A CRISPR screen of kinases and membrane proteins in mouse pancreatic cancer models identified tumor-intrinsic RIPK2 as a driver of immune evasion. Blocking RIPK2 restores surface MHC-I, loosens the dense fibrotic stroma and improves the response to anti-PD-1 therapy.

抑制细胞力感受器 FAK，使胰腺癌对检查点免疫治疗敏感Inhibiting the force sensor FAK makes pancreatic cancer responsive to checkpoint immunotherapy

抑制 FAK 可减轻肿瘤纤维化，减少免疫抑制细胞，促进 CD8⁺ T 细胞进入肿瘤，使原本对检查点抑制剂不敏感的胰腺癌获得响应。

FAK inhibition reduces fibrosis and immunosuppressive cells and lets CD8⁺ T cells enter the tumor, so a cancer that ignored checkpoint blockade starts to respond.

研究方向：胰腺癌Research directions: pancreatic cancer

四个方向都围绕胰腺导管腺癌（PDAC）：以临床问题为牵引，医工交叉，贯通从机制解析到治疗转化的全链条。 All four directions focus on pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC): driven by clinical problems, built across medicine and engineering, and spanning the full chain from mechanism to therapy.

解析Decode

胰腺癌力学–免疫空间图谱Mechano-immune tumor microenvironment spatial atlas of pancreatic cancer

用单细胞与空间组学构建胰腺癌全病程力学–免疫空间图谱，定量力学梯度与免疫排斥、耗竭的关系，鉴定力学信号响应细胞。 Single-cell and spatial profiling across the course of pancreatic cancer, quantifying how mechanical gradients relate to immune exclusion and exhaustion, and identifying force-responsive cells.

发现Discover

胰腺癌力学依赖性 CRISPR 筛选Mechanics-dependent CRISPR screening in pancreatic cancer

在 KPC 胰腺癌转基因小鼠和人胰腺癌类器官模型中，比较软、硬基质下的 CRISPR 筛选，鉴定分子开关并验证作用机制。 CRISPR screens on soft versus stiff matrices in KPC pancreatic cancer mouse models and human pancreatic cancer organoids to identify molecular switches and validate their mechanisms.

分层Stratify

胰腺癌病理组学与风险分层Pathomics and risk stratification in pancreatic cancer

建立胰腺癌力学微环境病理组学方法，构建力学–免疫风险分层模型，用分层结果指导胰腺癌患者的临床干预策略。 A pathomics method for the mechanical microenvironment of pancreatic cancer, a mechano-immune tumor microenvironment risk model, and stratification-guided clinical intervention for patients.

干预Intervene

胰腺癌工程化免疫细胞Engineered immune cells for pancreatic cancer

构建、优选并验证能适应胰腺癌力学微环境的工程化免疫细胞，推进细胞治疗。 Building, selecting and validating engineered immune cells suited to the mechanical microenvironment of pancreatic cancer.
